# Code of conduct

## TL;DR

- Treat contributors and users with dignity, patience, and professional care.
- Debate ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, retaliation, and disclosure of
  private information are unacceptable.
- Report conduct concerns privately whenever possible.

## Our commitment

Ergon contributors, maintainers, and community members commit to a welcoming,
safe, and equitable environment. Participation is open to people acting in good
faith regardless of identity, background, experience, ability, or viewpoint.
This policy is informed by the
[Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/).

## Expected and unacceptable behavior

Expected behavior includes empathy, constructive technical disagreement,
respect for boundaries, responsible handling of mistakes, and attention to the
effect of words and actions on others.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, stalking, discriminatory
or sexualized language or attention, sustained disruption, personal attacks,
retaliation, and publishing another person's private information without
permission. Technical criticism is welcome when specific, evidence-based, and
directed at the work.

## Scope, reporting, and enforcement

This policy applies in repository spaces and when someone officially represents
Ergon elsewhere. Report a concern privately to a maintainer through a private
contact method on the repository owner's GitHub profile. If none is available,
request one publicly without identifying people or describing the incident.

Maintainers should handle reports promptly, impartially, and as confidentially
as practical. They may remove content, issue a correction or warning, limit
participation, or ban participation in proportion to impact and pattern. A
maintainer involved in a report should recuse themselves where practical.
Retaliation is prohibited.
